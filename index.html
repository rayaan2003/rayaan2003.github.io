  • Architectural Mixing Strategies for Hybrid SSM-Attention Networks: A Systematic Study of Efficiency and Generalisation
    Rayaan Sheikh, supervised by Fredrik Dahlqvist
    MSc Dissertation, Queen Mary University of London, 2026
    paper / code / bibtex / tl;dr
    @mastersthesis{sheikh2026hybrid, title={Architectural Mixing Strategies for Hybrid SSM-Attention Networks: A Systematic Study of Efficiency and Generalisation}, author={Sheikh, Rayaan}, school={Queen Mary University of London}, year={2026} }
    We compare two ways of mixing a Transformer and a Mamba model. Stochastic routing (picking one model per input) provably collapses to whichever pure model is better. Probabilistic blending of the two output distributions is convex and reaches a genuine interior optimum that beats both baselines. We also measure a latency crossover at around 1,700 tokens, below which the Transformer is faster and above which Mamba wins.
